# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Koshora welcomes thoughtful, constructive participation. Be respectful, specific, and focused on improving the project. Technical disagreement is normal; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or deliberately disruptive behavior are not.

## Expected behavior

- give feedback on ideas and code, not people
- provide enough context to reproduce bugs
- respect privacy and never post real financial data, passwords, secret keys, or private user records
- disclose security issues privately using the process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)
- respect the Project Hub boundary and do not propose shortcuts that risk another application’s data

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory language, spam, intentionally unsafe changes, or disclosure of credentials/private financial records may result in removal from the project community.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users.
